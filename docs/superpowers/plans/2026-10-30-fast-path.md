# Fast replies without LLM generation

Goal: route exact greetings, thanks and "how are you" straight into the selected
TTS pipeline. Preserve cancellation, PCM credits, playback completion and history.

Use a small text whitelist with case/whitespace normalization and terminal
sentence punctuation. Do not use prefix matching. Compound commands, quoted text,
image requests and pending confirmation/selection flows keep their existing route.
Generate speech normally; do not add recordings or an audio cache in this change.
Keep background provider warmup independent of per-request generation.
